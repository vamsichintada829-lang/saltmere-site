1. THE GAME
=====================================================================
Title: Street Rush. Studio: Wheel Smart Studio. Package: com.wheelsmart.streetrush.
Genre: arcade drifting + capture the flag. 1 player (Solo Drift) or 2–4 players online (Team Code).
Android first: API 24+, target API 34+, IL2CPP, ARM64, landscape only, immersive full screen, screen kept on, internet permission.
Performance: 60 fps on mid-range Android, 30 fps minimum on low-end.

2. RULES (capture the flag)
=====================================================================
- Slot colours: 1 orange #FF6A0A, 2 blue #2F7BFF, 3 green #22C55E, 4 magenta #D23BD8. The car, base, base beam, pennant and name tag use the slot colour.
- A match has 10 rounds and NO time limit.
- Every round starts with a 10-second countdown. Cars are frozen at their spawns. Beeps at 3, 2, 1, then "GO!".
- The flag (metal pole + waving gold cloth with a wheel emblem, tall gold light beam) stands on a stand at the map centre.
- The first car within 3 m of the flag picks it up. The flag attaches to the car's rear roof and the gold beam follows it (hidden on the carrier's own screen).
- If any other car touches the carrier (centres within ~4.2 m), the flag instantly moves to that car. After any steal there is a 1.2 s cooldown before the next steal.
- Each player has a base: a 6.5 m radius circle in their colour with a colour beam, a pennant and a floating name tag. When the carrier enters their OWN base, the flag plants there, they score +1, there is a 3.5 s celebration, then the next round starts.
- Bases MOVE every round: each map has 12 base spots on the outer ring road (3 per side: middle, left, right), each with an inward direction toward the centre. The host picks one spot per player so that chosen spots are at least 150 m apart, and sends the choice to everyone. Cars spawn 14 m inward from their base, facing the centre. The flag resets to the centre.
- After round 10, the highest score wins: VICTORY / DEFEAT, or DRAW on a tie at the top.
- Solo Drift: the same rules alone, then show the total time ("10 flags in 3:42").
- HUD objective text: "GRAB THE FLAG" / "TAKE THE FLAG TO YOUR BASE" / "DASH <name> TO STEAL THE FLAG".
- Popups: GOT THE FLAG!, FLAG STOLEN!, FLAG LOST (<name> dashed you), <NAME> SCORES, POINT!, GO!.
- Do NOT add a navigation arrow above the car. The minimap is the only navigation aid.

3. MAPS (two scenes, each ~450 m square; boundary = invisible wall + painted line on the ground)
=====================================================================
Village Roads:
- Grass ground everywhere with large-scale colour variation (no visible tiling).
- A 5x5 network of 12 m wide dirt roads. Jitter interior intersections by about ±10 m so streets bend. Remove ~9 interior streets while keeping the network connected and the outer ring intact. Soft road edges, tyre ruts, pebbles. Round dirt patches at junctions.
- Centre: a dirt village ground with a South-Indian temple: stone plinth, white shrine with a red band, 5-tier coloured gopuram (cream / ochre / orange), gold kalasam on top, gold flagpole in front, small pillared porch. The flag stand is in front of the temple. The temple is solid.
- Up to 3 houses per street, facing the road: tiled-roof houses, flat-roof RCC houses (some 2-storey, some with a black water tank on the roof, parapets), round thatched huts. Pastel plastered walls, wooden doors, windows with sunshades, steps, plinth. Solid colliders. Never place houses on base spots, spawns, the temple ground or other roads.
- Nothing else on the playable area (no trees, poles or props).
City Streets:
- Paved concrete ground (large slabs with seams, stains, cracks).
- A 6x6 grid of 18 m wide tar roads: 2 lanes each way, a yellow double centre line, dashed white lane lines, edge lines, zebra crossings at every junction.
- A painted centre circle with the flag stand. Roads and ground only.
Generate both maps procedurally from a fixed random seed in the Editor builder, and save them as scenes with static batching. Store base spots, spawn points and the flag position in a MapDefinition ScriptableObject per map.
Minimap: round, top-left, rotates with the player. Shows roads, houses, bases in their colours, players as coloured dots, the flag as a gold icon and the local player as an arrow in their colour. Use a top-down orthographic camera to a RenderTexture with a simple unlit layer, or draw it in UI.

4. CAR AND DRIVING (custom arcade controller, NOT WheelColliders)
=====================================================================
- Rigidbody plus 4 raycast springs/dampers. Wheel meshes follow the ground and spin with speed.
- Split velocity into forward and lateral parts. Lateral grip per second: 9 normally, 2.4 while sliding (lateral speed > 4.5 m/s), 0.9 with the handbrake. Add back 35% of the lateral speed removed as forward speed (keeps drifts fast).
- Top speed 80 m/s with falloff near the top. Brake -32 m/s². Reverse capped at -15 m/s. Coasting drag ~2.2 m/s².
- Steering eases in and is reduced at high speed. Handbrake multiplies yaw rate by 1.7 above 8 m/s. Automatic 6-speed gearbox drives RPM for sound and the HUD gear.
- Collisions with cars and walls must NOT bounce: zero restitution along the contact normal, 3–6% extra speed damping, separate by half the penetration each. Strong hits only give camera shake, a thump sound and a brief screen flash.
- Drift (slip > 3.5 m/s at speed > 10 m/s):
  * body leans outward up to ~8.5°;
  * front wheels visually counter-steer toward the slide;
  * light trails in the car colour from both rear wheels (TrailRenderer, additive, ~0.55 s);
  * tyre smoke on tarmac, dust plus flying grit on dirt and grass;
  * skid-mark decals;
  * camera roll ±3°, FOV widens with speed, speed lines above 140 km/h;
  * a pulsing "DRIFT <points> xCombo" label.
- Car model: build a curved sporty coupe mesh in code (extruded side profile with bevels, glass canopy, rear wing; no boxy shapes) or leave a clearly marked slot for a licensed car model. Clear-coat paint in the slot colour, dark glass, emissive headlights and tail lights (brighter when braking), 5-spoke rims, brake discs, red calipers, blob shadow.
- Cameras (Cinemachine): chase (default), far chase, cinematic orbit (CAM button). The camera must never clip into houses or the temple (spherecast and pull in).
- Input System: touch buttons on screen and keyboard (arrows/WASD, Space = drift/handbrake, C = camera, Esc = pause).
