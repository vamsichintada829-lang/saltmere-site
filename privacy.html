Saltmere privacy policy

Effective 29 October 2026. Applies to the Saltmere game for Android.

The short version

Saltmere does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and the game does not send anything over the internet.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress on your phone only: the level you are on, your stars and coins, your boosters, the story repairs you have done, and whether sound is on. This never leaves your device. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes it.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, device identifiers, or how you play. Saltmere contains no third-party code that collects data: no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services.

Google Play

Google may collect information when you download the game from Google Play, under Google's privacy policy. We do not receive personal information from Google about who installs the game.

Children

Because Saltmere collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If a future version adds anything that collects data, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
